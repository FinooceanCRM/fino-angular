Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Finoocean collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the Finoocean application and website. It also explains your privacy choices and how to contact us.

We use personal information to provide and improve the service. Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on an appropriate basis, such as your consent where consent is required.

Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Finoocean. It may include information you provide directly, information generated while you use the service, and information you choose to submit through application features.

  • Account and contact information: name, email address, phone number, address, and account or role details.
  • Information you submit: loan lead and customer details, business or bank information, communications, notes, and documents or images uploaded to the service.
  • Usage and device information: IP address, browser and device type, operating system, pages and features used, access times, and diagnostic information. The mobile application may also use a device identifier or push-notification token to provide requested app features.

Please only submit personal information that you are authorized to provide, and use the service's access controls when sharing information with other users.

Device permissions

Some features may ask for permission to access your device's location, camera, or photo library. Location can be used for location-related features; camera or photo access can be used to capture or select documents and images. These permissions are used when you choose a feature that needs them, and you can manage or revoke them in your device settings. If you deny a permission, the related feature may not be available.

Information accessed through a feature may be uploaded to Finoocean's systems to provide that feature or may remain on your device, depending on the action you take.

Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use essential cookies or similar browser storage to support core functionality, such as maintaining a session and protecting accounts. Your browser or device settings let you manage or clear stored data; disabling it may affect some features. The mobile application may use local device storage to support its functionality.

How we use information

We may use information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and secure Finoocean.
  • Set up and administer accounts and provide role-based access.
  • Support loan lead workflows, customer records, bank matching, and communications between users involved in those workflows.
  • Respond to support requests and send service, security, and account communications, including verification codes where requested.
  • Diagnose problems, understand how the service is used, and improve its features and reliability.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect the rights, safety, and security of users and Finocean.

We send marketing communications only where permitted by applicable law. Where consent is required, we send them only with your consent. You may opt out of marketing messages using the instructions in the message or by contacting us.

When we share information

We may share information in these circumstances:

  • Service providers: with providers that help us operate the service, such as hosting, communications, and technical support providers, under appropriate safeguards.
  • Other users: information may be visible to users who are authorized to access it as part of their role or your use of a feature, such as a shared lead or customer workflow.
  • Business changes: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to applicable requirements.
  • Legal and safety reasons: when we reasonably believe disclosure is required by law or needed to protect rights, safety, or security, or to investigate potential wrongdoing.
  • With your direction or consent.

We do not sell mobile phone numbers or SMS consent records, or share mobile information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Providers that deliver messages may process the information needed to perform that service.

Text messages

If you provide your phone number and opt in where required, we may use it to send account verification codes, security alerts, account notifications, and support-related messages. Marketing messages are sent only as permitted by law and, where required, with your consent.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP where supported, or contact us for help. Opting out of non-essential messages does not prevent essential account or security communications where allowed by law.

Data retention and deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including providing the service, resolving disputes, protecting the service, and meeting legal or accounting obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and why it was collected. Information may remain in routine backups for a limited time before it is deleted or overwritten.

You may update account information through the service where those options are available. To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, email support@finoocean.com. We may need to retain certain information where required or permitted by law.

Security and transfers

We use reasonable measures designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Information may be processed in locations where we or our service providers operate. Where applicable law requires safeguards for transfers, we take steps intended to provide those safeguards.

Children's privacy

Finoocean is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action. Where applicable law sets a higher age or requires parental consent, we follow those requirements.

Links to other websites

Finoocean may link to third-party websites or services that we do not operate. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, which we encourage you to review. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party services.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the date above. If a change requires additional notice under applicable law, we will provide that notice through the service or by other appropriate means. Changes take effect when posted unless stated otherwise.
